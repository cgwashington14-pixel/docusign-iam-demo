Connect · Embed · Write-back

Keep people in your systems. Keep status honest.

DocuSign doesn’t have to be a destination. Embed signing and workflows in the portals agencies already use — then use Connect (webhooks) to push every status change back to the same record.

01

Embedded signing

Citizens, vendors, or staff sign inside Salesforce, a Power App, ServiceNow, or your agency portal — instead of dropping into a separate signing destination mid-process.

Agency portal
Addresses drop-off from context-switching · audit trail still in DocuSign
02

Embedded workflows

Launch Maestro / IAM workflows from the record — intake, approvals, and envelope generation — without rebuilding the same process in every host system.

  • Trigger from CRM, ITSM, or custom app
  • Map record data into workflow inputs
  • Return instance status to the host system
One governed process model · less custom workflow sprawl across agencies
03

DocuSign Connect — webhooks back to the record

As the envelope moves, Connect fires events your systems can consume. Status updates, completed documents, and recipient activity land on the same Salesforce, ServiceNow, or SharePoint record.

Connect event stream
  1. envelope-sent Recipients notified → Status: Sent
  2. envelope-delivered Signer opened packet → Status: Delivered
  3. envelope-completed All parties signed → Status: Completed · PDF attached
  4. recipient-completed Per-signer milestone → Update approver checklist

Beyond the first send

Iris extracts. Integrations deliver.

Not every agreement starts in a CRM. Vendor paper often arrives as a black box — teams hunt for parties, renewals, and risk by hand. Iris extracts those terms so they can pre-fill the next envelope or sync into Salesforce, ServiceNow, or SharePoint.

01

Ingest

Vendor paper or a prior MSA lands in Agreement Desk / CLM — the document becomes the source of truth.

02

Extract

Iris identifies parties, contract value, renewal dates, governing law, and risk clauses automatically.

03

Act

Pre-fill a renewal envelope, or push extracted fields back into the system of record your teams already use.
