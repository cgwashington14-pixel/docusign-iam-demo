Beyond the first send

Iris extracts. Integrations deliver.

Agreements don’t only start from a CRM record. DocuSign Iris can pull terms, parties, and obligations from an executed or third-party document — then that structured data can seed the next envelope or update Salesforce, ServiceNow, or SharePoint.

  1. Ingest — vendor paper or prior MSA lands in Agreement Desk / CLM
  2. Extract — Iris identifies parties, amounts, renewal dates, risk clauses
  3. Act — pre-fill a renewal envelope or push fields to the system of record
