Beyond the first send

Iris extracts. Integrations deliver.

Agreements don’t only start from a CRM record. DocuSign Iris pulls parties, terms, and obligations from executed or third-party paper — then that structured data can seed the next envelope or update Salesforce, ServiceNow, or SharePoint.

01

Ingest

Vendor paper or a prior MSA lands in Agreement Desk / CLM — the document becomes the source of truth.

02

Extract

Iris identifies parties, contract value, renewal dates, governing law, and risk clauses automatically.

03

Act

Pre-fill a renewal envelope, or push extracted fields back into the system of record your teams already use.
